# User Guide

FireSight AI is organized around daily forest-fire intelligence workflows.

## Dashboard

Use the dashboard to review overall risk, active hotspots, weather pressure, and high-priority alerts.

## Map

Use GIS layers to inspect risk GeoJSON, hotspots, vegetation overlays, and regional spatial statistics.

## Prediction

Submit weather, vegetation, and hotspot conditions to generate a wildfire risk score, category, confidence, explanation, and recommendations.

## Alerts

Review alert severity, explanation, recommended actions, acknowledgement state, and resolution status.

## Reports

Generate prediction, GIS, weather, vegetation, analytics, executive, or incident reports. Supported export contracts include PDF, CSV, Excel, JSON, and GeoJSON.

## Settings

Review operational thresholds, notification preferences, and refresh intervals. Administrative changes require the proper role.
